What We Do

We excel at underwriting commercial mortgage loans at origination, at securitization, and for B Piece Buyers of Commercial Mortgage-Backed Securities.

Other services include property site inspections, lease abstracts, appraisal reviews, mortgage banker packages, and special servicing.

Overview

We have completed over $15 billion of contract due diligence consisting of various property types located in over three-quarters of the United States since 2002.

Properties include multifamily, retail, office, industrial, hotel, self-storage, and mobile home parks. Asset sizes range from $1 million to $250 million portfolios.

In 2007, InvestWood was selected for the “Aggie 100” list of fastest growing companies.
